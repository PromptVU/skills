# Tailor a demo for a meeting

Read first: `references/rules.md` and `references/writing-prompts.md`.

Steps in the PromptVu app are written as **bold actions**. How to do each one is in `references/promptvu-app.md`.

## What to do

### 1. Get what you need
Ask once, as a short list, for anything missing:

- The sequence to start from
- Who they're meeting: name, role, company
- What the buyer cares about: top two or three priorities
- Objections they expect
- What they want out of the call: the next step
- The buyer's website

Start from the rep's saved "[PRODUCT] knowledge base". If there isn't one, tell the rep and offer to run `promptvu-onboard` first. You can continue with what they give you, but say the prompts will be weaker.

### 2. Research the buyer
Read only. Look at the buyer's website, recent news and the person's role. If the rep has connected call recordings or a CRM to you, use `promptvu-build-from-calls` for that part.

Write a short buyer brief:

- What the company does, size, industry
- What this person owns and is measured on
- The two or three pains this demo should hit, in the buyer's words where you have them
- The ROI angle that matters most to them

### 3. Plan the rewrite
**Get into PromptVu**, **Find a sequence** and **Read all prompts**. For each prompt, decide:

- The new text: the buyer's problem, what this screen does about it, the result. One or two sentences, in the rep's voice, with one specific.
- Whether a page needs a new prompt.
- Whether a page doesn't help this buyer. Don't remove it. Suggest skipping it on the call.

Give every prompt on a page its own wording.

### 4. Make the copy
**Duplicate a sequence.** Work only in the copy.

### 5. Rewrite
Go prompt by prompt in the copy and **Edit a prompt's text**. Keep every prompt on its page and spot.

For new prompts, **Play a sequence** (the copy), **Move between pages** to the page, then **Add a prompt on the page**, or **Add a prompt from the PromptVu Manager** for text boxes.

### 6. Check
**Check placement** for every prompt. **Reset a missing prompt** if one doesn't show.

### 7. Report
Give the rep:

1. The copy's name, exactly as it shows in Prompt Sequences
2. A three-line buyer brief
3. Every change, by prompt number and page, with old and new text
4. Pages you'd skip, and why
5. Facts you need confirmed before the call
