Questions from sales leaders

Will my reps sound scripted?+

No. Notes are reminders, not teleprompters: a number to quote, a question to ask, the next screen to show. Reps say it their way.

Can I control the team version?+

Yes. Team sequences live in a shared library, and roles for admins, managers and reps control who manages them.

Can buyers see the notes?+

No. Reps share the clean Presenter Screen. Markers and notes stay on their own screen.
