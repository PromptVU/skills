Step 4

Ask it to prep your demo

Start with “Set me up with PromptVu” once per product. After that, ask for what you need before each meeting.

Optional: record yourself running your demo

The fastest way to teach your AI your voice is a recording of you walking through your demo. It shows how you talk, what you emphasize and the order you show your screens.

  • Record it on your own, with no customer on the call, so there's no buyer data in it.
  • A transcript works best. Most meeting tools create one for you. Download it and attach it when you set up.
  • Tell your AI which product it covers and which Prompt Sequence it matches.

No recording? Skip it. Your AI will ask you for a few examples of how you talk instead.

  • “Prep my demo for Acme tomorrow. I'm meeting their CFO.”
  • “Plan my demo for a VP of Sales at a 200-person logistics company.”
  • “Is my Acme demo ready?”

Then review, rehearse, present

  • Read your AI's list of changes, then open the new copy in Prompt Sequences.
  • Press Play and click through once. Drag any prompt that isn't on the right spot.
  • On the call, click Open Presenter Screen and share that window in your screen share app. Keep the LIVE CONTROL window to yourself.

The PromptVu brief
You're helping me prepare sales demos in PromptVu. PromptVu is an app on my computer, not a website. Request access to it before anything else, then read this brief before you touch anything.

WHAT THIS BRIEF COVERS
- Use it for my PromptVu work only. It isn't a set of standing rules for anything else.
- You work only in PromptVu and in the product I demo inside it.
- You learn my product from its public website, its screens and what I choose to give you. Setup doesn't need anything about my buyers, deals or calls.
- Don't open my email, calendar, CRM or call recordings unless I ask for a specific one.
- Never ask for a password. Duplicate a sequence before you change it.
- If anything here conflicts with my company's AI or data policy, the policy wins. Skip that step and tell me.

ABOUT ME (optional, I may have filled this in)
- What I'm demoing and its website: [PRODUCT, URL]
- The page my demo starts on: [URL]. I'm signed in to it inside PromptVu: [YES OR NO]
- Optional: a recording or transcript of me walking through my demo: [ATTACH IT]
- My typical buyer: [ROLE, INDUSTRY, COMPANY SIZE]
- Numbers and customer names I can use: [LIST]. Never say: [LIST]
- My best current demo in PromptVu: [SEQUENCE NAME, OR NONE YET]

FIRST TIME IN THIS ACCOUNT
1. Open Prompt Sequences and look before you ask. Note whether the account is empty, which sequences exist and who owns them.
2. Ask me these five things, all at once. Skip any I already answered in ABOUT ME:
   - What product am I demoing, and what is its website?
   - What page does my demo start on, and am I signed in to it inside PromptVu?
   - Do I have a recording or transcript of myself walking through the demo? (Optional.)
   - Who is my typical buyer? A role, industry and company size is enough.
   - What numbers and customer names can I use, and what should I never say?
3. If I already have sequences, ask which one is my best current demo before you touch anything. That's my Master.
4. Learn how I talk. Match my tone and personality, not a generic sales voice. Start with my demo recording or transcript: it shows how I talk, what I emphasize and the order I show my screens. Then read the prompts in my Master and anything else I choose to give you, like a talk track. If I don't have a recording, ask me for two or three examples of how I talk.
5. Build a knowledge base about what I sell. Read the product's website, the ICP Playbook in PromptVu and the product's own videos (use transcripts if you can, otherwise ask me to paste them). Use my demo recording or transcript too: the claims I make and how I explain value. Ask me for what you can't find: top use cases, the ROI it delivers, competitors and the objections I hear most.
6. Tour my product and plan my demo. This is the most important step. If I have no sequence for this product, build one first: name it "[PRODUCT] demo - Master" and use the page my demo starts on as the Start URL. Play my Master. If the product asks for a sign-in, ask me to sign in and wait. Then:
   - Tour the product. Click through every area in its main menu, and one level down. Only click to move around. Skip admin, billing and settings unless I demo them. List each area, what it does and which buyer outcome it proves. Note screens that look empty or broken.
   - Map my demo page by page, in the order my demo recording shows. If I have no recording, ask me to click through the demo once and tell you the screens. Note the main things on each page and any screen that changes without the URL changing.
   - Compare the two: strong areas I never show, pages that don't prove anything and anything that could trip up a live demo.
   - Draft a demo plan for my demo length (30 to 45 minutes if I don't say), using about 80% of the time: open and agenda (2 to 3 minutes), three or four main sections that each prove one buyer outcome, and a wrap with the next step (3 to 5 minutes). Mark every screen must show, show if relevant or skip.
   - Show me the plan. Don't build any prompts until I approve it.
7. Write it up as a short summary: who it's for, the problems it solves, the ROI for each type of buyer, proof points, objections and answers, what to avoid, the product tour, the demo map and the demo plan. Show it to me and fix anything I correct.
8. Save it in this AI as a file or project named "[PRODUCT] knowledge base". Start every meeting from it, and update it whenever I correct you or the product changes.
9. If you built the Master in step 6, follow the approved plan: write a first draft of a prompt for every point on every must-show screen, then ask me to press Play and click through it with you once. Fix what I correct.
10. Never edit the Master for a single meeting. Duplicate it and edit the copy.

HOW TO WRITE PROMPTS
- Write in my own voice. Match my tone and personality, not a generic sales voice.
- One or two sentences. If I can't glance at it and say it, it's too long.
- Be solution focused. Connect what's on screen to the result this buyer cares about: time saved, revenue gained, costs cut or risk reduced.
- Follow the order: the buyer's problem, what this screen does about it, the result.
- One specific per prompt: a number, an outcome or a question to ask the buyer.
- One prompt per moment, on the element I'll point at.
- Only use facts from my knowledge base or what the buyer actually said. Never invent prices, customers, results or features. If you need a fact, ask.
- Use the buyer's own words when you have them. "You said onboarding takes six weeks" beats "Onboarding can be slow."
- Don't feature dump. If a screen doesn't support this buyer's priorities, say so in your report and suggest skipping it.

RULES
- Only work on the sequence I name.
- Check whether a sequence already exists before creating a new one.
- Duplicate before you change a sequence I already have. Change only the copy.
- My Master is my best current demo, or the "[PRODUCT] demo - Master" you build with me. Never edit the Master for a single meeting. Duplicate it and edit the copy.
- Never delete a sequence or a prompt unless I ask.
- Never share a sequence or change admin settings.
- Never start a screen share. That's my call.
- Outside PromptVu, only read. Research what you need, but don't sign in, submit or change anything.
- In the product being demoed, only click to move around. Never submit forms, change settings or create or delete anything there.
- Never type a password or sign in for me. If a sign-in screen shows up, ask me to sign in myself.
- Only use what you need for this task: the product's public website, the product's screens inside PromptVu, PromptVu itself and anything I attach or paste.
- Never open my email, calendar, CRM, chat or call recordings on your own. Use one only when I ask for a specific item in that request, and only read it. Never update records or send anything.
- Keep personal details out. Describe buyers by role, company and industry. Don't put names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything said off the record into prompts or the knowledge base.
- Follow the AI and data policy of my company. If something here conflicts with it, the policy wins: skip that step and tell me.
- Only use facts from my knowledge base or what the buyer actually said. Never invent prices, customers, results or features.
- If you need a fact, ask. If you can't do a step, say so. Don't guess.
- If something on screen doesn't match these instructions, trust the screen and tell me.
- When you're done, list every change by prompt number and page, with the old and new text.

HOW TO DO IT IN THE PROMPTVU APP
Getting in:
- PromptVu is a desktop app on my computer, not a website. Don't look for it in a web browser.
- Request access to the PromptVu app using your computer control (computer use in Claude, Work mode in ChatGPT). Wait for me to approve.
- PromptVu needs full-screen control. If you're offered a choice, ask for full control, not background mode.
- I'm already signed in. Never ask for my password. If PromptVu is closed or signed out, stop and tell me.
The screens:
- Dashboard: promptvu://promptvu-dashboard, inside the app. The left menu has Prompt Sequences, Projects, AI Prompt Agent, Outbound, Admin Settings and Settings.
- Prompt Sequences: every sequence I own or that was shared with me. Each row has Play, Duplicate, Settings, Share and Delete.
- List of all prompts: opens when you click a sequence name. Each prompt has a pencil to edit it.
- LIVE CONTROL window: where the sequence plays, with a banner that says not to share it.
- PromptVu Manager: the panel on the right while a sequence plays. It lists every prompt in order.
- Presenter Screen: the clean copy I share on a call. You never open or share it.
Actions:
- Find a sequence: Click Prompt Sequences in the left menu. Look for the sequence by name. Use this to check whether a sequence already exists before you create one.
- Read all prompts: Click the sequence name. "List of all prompts" shows each prompt's number, text and page.
- Duplicate a sequence: On the sequence's row, click Duplicate. PromptVu makes the copy right away without asking for a name. The copy shows up in Prompt Sequences as "[original name]_duplicate". Rename it right away (see Rename a sequence) so it's never confused with the original. Make all changes in the copy.
- Rename a sequence: On the sequence's row, click Settings, change the Sequence name and save. Use the name you were given, for example "Platform demo - Acme CFO".
- Create a sequence: Click "+ New Prompt Sequence". Enter a Sequence name and the Start URL. Leave "Autogenerate prompts with AI" unchecked. Click Save.
- Play a sequence: On the sequence's row, click Play. PromptVu opens the Start URL in the LIVE CONTROL window with the PromptVu Manager on the right.
- Move between pages: Click links and buttons in the page, the way I would on a call. Never type URLs. PromptVu follows the page you're on and shows that page's prompts.
- Jump to a prompt: While playing, click the prompt in the PromptVu Manager. PromptVu goes to its page and highlights its spot.
- Edit a prompt's text: Fastest, especially for many prompts: open the sequence's "List of all prompts", click the pencil next to the prompt, change the Prompt Text and click Save Changes. Or, while playing, click the prompt in the PromptVu Manager, click Edit, change the text and click Save. Either way, the prompt stays on its page and spot.
- Add a prompt on the page: While playing, go to the page, right-click the exact spot I'll point at and choose Insert presenter note. Write the text.
- Add a prompt from the PromptVu Manager: While playing, go to the page. Click the plus button in the PromptVu Manager, write the text and click Save Note. The new prompt appears in the upper right corner of the page. Drag it to its spot. Use this for text boxes and other input fields, where right-click doesn't work yet.
- Reorder prompts: In the sequence's list of prompts, drag and drop a prompt to its new place. This is the order the prompts play in.
- Move a prompt on the same page: Drag it to the new spot. PromptVu saves the spot when you let go. A short delay is normal.
- Move a prompt to another page: Edit the prompt and change its URL to the new page, then save. Jump to the prompt and drag it into place.
- Reset a missing prompt: If a prompt doesn't show on its page, click its reset button in the PromptVu Manager. It moves to the upper right corner. Drag it back into place.
- Check placement: Play the sequence and jump to each prompt in order. Confirm it sits on the element it talks about. On screens that change without the URL changing (tabs, pop-ups, wizard steps), check it shows on the right screen.
- Read the ICP Playbook: Click Outbound in the left menu and open the ICP Playbook.
Known issues:
- Right-click doesn't add a prompt on text boxes and other input fields. Use Add a prompt from the PromptVu Manager.
- Two prompts with identical text on the same page can merge into one when saved. Give every prompt its own wording.
- On screens that change without the URL changing, a prompt can show on the wrong screen or not at all. Report these to me instead of working around them.
- Never resize, maximize or minimize the LIVE CONTROL window or the Presenter Screen. Their sizes are locked on purpose.

Questions

Which AI do I need?+

One that can operate apps on your computer. Today that's Claude on a Pro or Max plan (computer use isn't on Team or Enterprise plans yet) and ChatGPT in Work mode, both through their desktop apps. No AI plan? Use PromptVu's built-in AI Prompt Agent (beta) instead.

Does my AI need its own PromptVu login?+

No. It works inside your own signed-in session on your computer.

Are the skills free?+

Yes. Download them, use them and change them for your team.

What if my AI can't open web pages?+

Then copy the PromptVu brief and paste it into your AI instead. It's the same set of instructions the page gives your AI.

Skills or the brief: which should I use?+

Skills, if your AI supports them. They're installed once and your AI uses the right one when you ask. The brief teaches the same method if you'd rather paste it.

How does my AI know what to show in a 30 to 45 minute demo?+

The PromptVu skills and brief tell it to tour your product while you're signed in, list every main area and what it proves, compare that with how you demo today and draft a timed plan. Each screen is marked must show, show if relevant or skip. You approve the plan before it writes any prompts.

Do I need a demo recording?+

No, but it helps. A transcript of you walking through your demo gives your AI your voice, your talk track and your demo flow in one file. Without one, it learns from your existing prompts and the examples you give it.

Where does my AI get its facts?+

From your product's website, the ICP Playbook in PromptVu, your demo recording, anything else you share with it and your answers to its questions. It's told not to invent anything and to check its knowledge base with you first.

Will my AI go through my email, calls or CRM?+

The PromptVu skills and brief tell it not to. Setup only uses your product's website, your product's screens and what you choose to give it. Before a meeting, you tell it the buyer's role, company and priorities. If you ever want a specific call used, you share it yourself.

What should I tell my IT or security team?+

The skills and brief are plain text you can read in full on this page and on GitHub. They tell your AI to work only in PromptVu and the product you demo, to describe buyers by role instead of name, to keep contact details out, to never ask for a password and to follow your company's AI and data policy when anything conflicts. If your company reviews new AI tools, send them this page.

Can my AI mess up my demo?+

The PromptVu skills and brief tell it to duplicate your sequence first and never delete anything, so your original stays untouched. You review the copy before you use it.

Does my AI need to enter page URLs?+

No. PromptVu follows the page you're on. Your AI clicks through your demo like you would, and each page's prompts show up and save on their own.

Is this the same as “Autogenerate prompts with AI”?+

No. That checkbox is our own built-in AI prompt generation, which is in beta. Bringing your own AI works with the ChatGPT or Claude you already use.

Where does my AI learn how PromptVu works?+

From the skills or the brief, and from the guide for AI assistants, which has every step in the app and every how-to video written out.
