How to use PromptVu: a guide for AI assistants

Everything an AI needs to operate PromptVu for a rep: what to do, the rules, how to write prompts that sell, how to do each step in the app, and every how-to video written out.

Point your AI here: https://promptvu.com/ai-guide.html

Who this page is for

This page is written for AI assistants, like ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, that operate PromptVu for a rep. If you're a person, start at Use PromptVu with your AI. If a rep asked you to set them up, go to that page and follow the steps for AI assistants.

You work in PromptVu only by controlling the PromptVu desktop app on the rep's computer, with the app open and the rep signed in.

This page has three parts. What to do is in the PromptVu skills and the rules. How to do it in the app is the app section: which screen and which button. The video lessons are every PromptVu how-to video, written out.

For how PromptVu works, use only this page and what the rep tells you. For facts about the product the rep sells, use the rep's knowledge base. If something here doesn't match what you see on screen, trust the screen and tell the rep.

What to do: the PromptVu skills

Each skill is a step-by-step method. Steps in the app are written as bold actions, and each action is explained in the app section of this page.

  • Onboard PromptVu: Looks at your account, asks you five questions, learns your voice, builds a knowledge base about what you sell and maps your demo. Builds your Master demo if you don't have one.
  • Map and plan your demo: Tours your product, maps your demo page by page and drafts a timed 30 to 45 minute demo plan for you to approve.
  • Tailor for a meeting: Researches the buyer, duplicates your Prompt Sequence and rewrites every prompt in your voice around their ROI.
  • Build from a call: Builds the demo around what the buyer said, from a call transcript or notes you choose to share.
  • Build a big demo: Maps the buying committee, writes the run-of-show and builds one sequence for the group plus one per stakeholder.
  • Check a sequence: Checks every prompt for voice, ROI, facts and placement, plays it through and tells you what to fix.

Install them from GitHub or Use PromptVu with your AI.

Rules for every PromptVu skill

These are about what to do, not how. They don't change when the app changes.

Protect the rep's work

  • Only work on the sequence the rep names.
  • Check whether a sequence already exists before creating a new one.
  • Duplicate before you change a sequence the rep already has. Change only the copy.
  • The rep's Master is their best current demo, or the "[PRODUCT] demo - Master" you build with them. Never edit the Master for a single meeting. Duplicate it and edit the copy.
  • Never delete a sequence or a prompt unless the rep asks.
  • Never share a sequence or change admin settings.
  • Never start a screen share. That's the rep's call.

Stay read-only everywhere else

  • Outside PromptVu, only read. Research what you need, but don't sign in, submit or change anything.
  • In the product being demoed, only click to move around. Never submit forms, change settings or create or delete anything there.
  • Never type a password or sign in for the rep. If a sign-in screen shows up, ask the rep to sign in themselves.

Respect the rep's data

  • Only use what you need for this task: the product's public website, the product's screens inside PromptVu, PromptVu itself and anything the rep attaches or pastes.
  • Never open the rep's email, calendar, CRM, chat or call recordings on your own. Use one only when the rep asks for a specific item in that request, and only read it. Never update records or send anything.
  • Keep personal details out. Describe buyers by role, company and industry. Don't put names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything said off the record into prompts or the knowledge base.
  • Follow the AI and data policy of the rep's company. If something here conflicts with it, the policy wins: skip that step and tell the rep.

Be honest

  • Only use facts from the rep's knowledge base or what the buyer actually said. Never invent prices, customers, results or features.
  • If you need a fact, ask. If you can't do a step, say so. Don't guess.
  • If something on screen doesn't match these instructions, trust the screen and tell the rep.

Report

  • When you're done, list every change by prompt number and page, with the old and new text.

Writing prompts that sell

A prompt is what the rep will say at that moment on the call. Write for the ear, not the page.

The rules

  • Write in the rep's own voice. Match their tone and personality, not a generic sales voice.
  • One or two sentences. If the rep can't glance at it and say it, it's too long.
  • Be solution focused. Connect what's on screen to the result this buyer cares about: time saved, revenue gained, costs cut or risk reduced.
  • Follow the order: the buyer's problem, what this screen does about it, the result.
  • One specific per prompt: a number, an outcome or a question to ask the buyer.
  • One prompt per moment, on the element the rep will point at.
  • Only use facts from the rep's knowledge base or what the buyer actually said. Never invent prices, customers, results or features. If you need a fact, ask.
  • Use the buyer's own words when you have them. "You said onboarding takes six weeks" beats "Onboarding can be slow."
  • Don't feature dump. If a screen doesn't support this buyer's priorities, say so in your report and suggest skipping it.

Weak vs strong

Weak Strong
"This is our pricing page. We have three plans." "You said reps lose six hours a week to manual reporting. Business gives that back, which covers the cost in the first quarter."
"Here's the dashboard with lots of great metrics." "This is the one number your VP asked about on our last call: pipeline created per rep. Watch it update live."
"We integrate with many tools." "You're on Salesforce, so this syncs both ways. No one re-enters a deal."

Questions are prompts too

Some of the best prompts are a question the rep asks at that moment:

  • "Before I click into this, how are you handling approvals today?"
  • "Is this the report your CFO would want, or would she cut it differently?"

Use a question when the rep needs the buyer to confirm pain before showing the answer.

How to do it in the PromptVu app

Only this part says which screen and which button. The skills and rules say what to do. When a skill names an action in bold, find that action here.

You work in PromptVu by controlling the PromptVu desktop app on the rep's computer, with the app open and the rep signed in. There is no other way in.

Get into PromptVu

  • PromptVu is a desktop app on the rep's computer, not a website. Don't look for it in a web browser.
  • Request access to the PromptVu app using your computer control (computer use in Claude, Work mode in ChatGPT). Wait for the rep to approve.
  • PromptVu needs full-screen control. If you're offered a choice, ask for full control, not background mode.
  • The rep must already be signed in. Never ask for their password. If PromptVu is closed or signed out, stop and tell the rep.

Words PromptVu uses

  • Prompt: a short script attached to an exact spot on a web page. It tells the rep what to show and what to say at that moment. PromptVu remembers the page and the spot.
  • Prompt Sequence: a whole demo. The prompts, in order, across every page the rep presents, starting from a Start URL.
  • Start URL: the page a Prompt Sequence opens on when you press Play.
  • Owner: the person who created a sequence. Only the owner can edit it. Anyone it's shared with can run it or duplicate it.
  • Projects, AI Prompt Agent and Outbound: PromptVu's own built-in AI features, all in beta. Outbound holds the ICP Playbook, which is useful context about who the rep sells to.

The screens

  • Dashboard: promptvu://promptvu-dashboard, inside the app. The left menu has Prompt Sequences, Projects, AI Prompt Agent, Outbound, Admin Settings and Settings.
  • Prompt Sequences: every sequence the rep owns or that was shared with them. Each row has Play, Duplicate, Settings, Share and Delete.
  • List of all prompts: opens when you click a sequence name. Each prompt has a pencil to edit it.
  • LIVE CONTROL window: where the sequence plays, with a banner that says not to share it.
  • PromptVu Manager: the panel on the right while a sequence plays. It lists every prompt in order.
  • Presenter Screen: the clean copy the rep shares on a call. You never open or share it.

Actions

Find a sequence

Click Prompt Sequences in the left menu. Look for the sequence by name. Use this to check whether a sequence already exists before you create one.

Read all prompts

Click the sequence name. "List of all prompts" shows each prompt's number, text and page.

Duplicate a sequence

On the sequence's row, click Duplicate. PromptVu makes the copy right away without asking for a name. The copy shows up in Prompt Sequences as "[original name]_duplicate". Rename it right away (see Rename a sequence) so it's never confused with the original. Make all changes in the copy.

Rename a sequence

On the sequence's row, click Settings, change the Sequence name and save. Use the name you were given, for example "Platform demo - Acme CFO".

Create a sequence

Click "+ New Prompt Sequence". Enter a Sequence name and the Start URL. Leave "Autogenerate prompts with AI" unchecked. Click Save.

Play a sequence

On the sequence's row, click Play. PromptVu opens the Start URL in the LIVE CONTROL window with the PromptVu Manager on the right.

Move between pages

Click links and buttons in the page, the way the rep would on a call. Never type URLs. PromptVu follows the page you're on and shows that page's prompts.

Jump to a prompt

While playing, click the prompt in the PromptVu Manager. PromptVu goes to its page and highlights its spot.

Edit a prompt's text

Fastest, especially for many prompts: open the sequence's "List of all prompts", click the pencil next to the prompt, change the Prompt Text and click Save Changes. Or, while playing, click the prompt in the PromptVu Manager, click Edit, change the text and click Save. Either way, the prompt stays on its page and spot.

Add a prompt on the page

While playing, go to the page, right-click the exact spot the rep will point at and choose Insert presenter note. Write the text.

Add a prompt from the PromptVu Manager

While playing, go to the page. Click the plus button in the PromptVu Manager, write the text and click Save Note. The new prompt appears in the upper right corner of the page. Drag it to its spot. Use this for text boxes and other input fields, where right-click doesn't work yet.

Reorder prompts

In the sequence's list of prompts, drag and drop a prompt to its new place. This is the order the prompts play in.

Move a prompt on the same page

Drag it to the new spot. PromptVu saves the spot when you let go. A short delay is normal.

Move a prompt to another page

Edit the prompt and change its URL to the new page, then save. Jump to the prompt and drag it into place.

Reset a missing prompt

If a prompt doesn't show on its page, click its reset button in the PromptVu Manager. It moves to the upper right corner. Drag it back into place.

Check placement

Play the sequence and jump to each prompt in order. Confirm it sits on the element it talks about. On screens that change without the URL changing (tabs, pop-ups, wizard steps), check it shows on the right screen.

Read the ICP Playbook

Click Outbound in the left menu and open the ICP Playbook.

Known issues today

  • Right-click doesn't add a prompt on text boxes and other input fields. Use Add a prompt from the PromptVu Manager.
  • Two prompts with identical text on the same page can merge into one when saved. Give every prompt its own wording.
  • On screens that change without the URL changing, a prompt can show on the wrong screen or not at all. Report these to the rep instead of working around them.
  • Never resize, maximize or minimize the LIVE CONTROL window or the Presenter Screen. Their sizes are locked on purpose.

How to Tailor a Prompt Sequence with Your Own AI

Watch on YouTube · 2:17

  • Great reps know their product and the buyer's job, team, business and strategy. PromptVu uses your AI to bring the two together.
  • On screen, the PromptVu Manager is on the right and the prompts sit on the page.
  • Tell your AI who you're meeting and what they care about. For example: "I'm meeting with the CFO of a large toy company. They want a high-level view of the product and the case for buying instead of building in house. Build the Prompt Sequence."
  • In the video, the AI duplicates the original sequence and tailors the prompts and talk track in the copy, using what it knows about the product. The original stays the same.
  • Work that used to take hours of prep takes a few minutes. Review the copy before you present.

How to Edit and Save Prompts in PromptVu

Watch on YouTube · 2:06

  • The PromptVu Manager opens on the right whenever a sequence is playing. It shows every prompt and helps you move between them.
  • Click a prompt in the PromptVu Manager to highlight its spot on the page, or jump to its page.
  • Each prompt remembers where it was saved, even further down a page.
  • To change the text, click the prompt in the PromptVu Manager, click Edit, type the new script and click Save. The page updates right away.
  • To place a new prompt, click Insert presenter note and drag it into place. Edit its text in the PromptVu Manager.
  • To move a prompt to another page, edit its URL and save. It leaves the current page, and clicking it in the PromptVu Manager takes you to the new page.

How to Reset and Reposition Prompts in PromptVu

Watch on YouTube · 2:19

  • PromptVu reads each website in real time to keep every prompt on its exact spot.
  • New prompts from the PromptVu Manager appear in the upper right corner. Drag them into place and PromptVu saves the spot.
  • A short delay after dragging is normal. PromptVu is saving the spot.
  • If a prompt disappears, the website's layout or URL may have changed. Click the reset button for that prompt in the PromptVu Manager. It returns to the upper right corner so you can drag it back into place.

How to Use the Presenter Screen in PromptVu

Watch on YouTube · 2:17

  • The Presenter Screen lets you present from your prompts while the audience sees a clean page with only your navigation.
  • Press Play on a sequence. The PromptVu Manager opens on the right with your prompts in order.
  • The banner at the top says this is the control window and not to share it. It hides when you hover over it.
  • When you're ready, click Open Presenter Screen. It mirrors your mouse and every click from your control window.
  • The audience never sees the prompts on the Presenter Screen.
  • Share the Presenter Screen window in Zoom or any other meeting app.

Presenter Mode: Why the Window Size Is Locked

Watch on YouTube · 1:34

  • PromptVu runs a second window, the Presenter Screen, so you can share a clean screen while you work from another.
  • To keep both in sync, the Presenter Screen and your control window must be exactly the same size, whether you use one screen or two.
  • The Presenter Screen's size is locked. Don't try to resize, maximize or minimize it.
  • That keeps your mouse and clicks in the same place on your screen and the audience's.

How to Share Prompt Sequences with Your Team in PromptVu

Watch on YouTube · 1:18

  • To share a sequence, click Share on it and type a teammate's email.
  • If they have a PromptVu account, they're notified and the sequence shows up in their list, with you as the owner.
  • Sequences shared with you can be run but not edited. Only the owner can edit them.
  • To make your own version, duplicate (clone) it. Your edits go into your copy.
  • When the owner edits their sequence, the shared version updates too.

Share your screen, not your playbook.
