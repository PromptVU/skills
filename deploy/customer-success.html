Questions from customer success

Can we use it on calls with existing customers?+

Yes. Use it for any call where you share your screen: kickoffs, trainings, check-ins, QBRs and renewals.

Can a new CSM run onboarding in week one?+

Yes. They present from the team's sequences, with the talk track on the screen they're showing.

Does the customer see the prompts?+

No. CSMs share the clean Presenter Screen. Prompts stay on their own screen.
