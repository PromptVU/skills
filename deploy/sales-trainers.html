The problem

Training that fades after the workshop is hard to renew

Clients want proof in win rates and pipeline, not attendance. That proof is decided on live calls, where you aren't.

Today

  • Reps forget most of a workshop within weeks and slip back to old habits
  • Clients judge you on win rates, but you can't be on their calls
  • AI practice tools are competing for the same training budget
  • Your engagement ends when the workshop does

With PromptVu

  • Your questions and talk tracks sit on the screens reps share with buyers
  • Your methodology runs on every live call, even when you're not in the room
  • Practice tools prepare reps, and PromptVu backs them up on the real call
  • You stay in the account with a reason to keep working together
How it works

Build it once, deliver it to every client

  1. 1

    Build your methodology once

    Turn your frameworks, questions and talk tracks into Prompt Sequences in your own PromptVu account.

  2. 2

    Tailor it to each client

    Copy your master sequence and fit it to the client's product, buyers and pricing. Your AI can do the first pass with our setup guide.

  3. 3

    Push it to every client team

    Send sequences into each client's account from one place. When you improve the master, push the new version.

  4. 4

    Reps run it on live calls

    Prompts sit on the exact screen the rep is sharing. Only the rep sees them. The buyer sees a clean demo, and the methodology shows up in the call recording.

Questions from sales trainers

Does PromptVu replace my training?+

No. You still teach. PromptVu delivers your methodology on live calls, so what you teach shows up after the workshop.

Can I manage several clients from one account?+

Yes. Build a sequence once and push it into each client's PromptVu account. When you improve it, push the update.

How do I know reps are using it?+

Listen to the call recordings. When reps follow the prompts, you hear your methodology in the conversation with the buyer.

Does it work with my clients' screen share apps?+

Yes. Reps share the clean Presenter Screen in Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or any other screen share app.

How does the referral program work?+

Training partners can refer their client teams to PromptVu through our referral program. Book a call and we'll walk you through it.
