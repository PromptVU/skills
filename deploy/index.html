Questions

Can my audience see my notes?+

No. You share the clean Presenter Screen; markers and notes stay on yours.

Why is it a download?+

A browser tab can't hide notes from a screen share. PromptVu is built on Chromium so it can keep a private presenter layer.

Does it work with my screen share app?+

Yes. PromptVu works with any screen share app, like Zoom, Google Meet or Teams. Share the Presenter Screen like you normally would. Your prompts stay on your screen.

Do I have to use AI?+

No. Prompts are yours to write. Having your own ChatGPT or Claude update them for a meeting is optional.

Does Outbound send emails for me?+

No. Outbound is in beta. You approve every message, and approved messages go to your own sequencer.
