Questions from RevOps

Does it work with our product?+

If you present it in a browser, yes. PromptVu pins notes to any web page or web app.

Why is PromptVu a download?+

A browser tab can't hide notes from a screen share. PromptVu is built on Chromium so it can keep a private presenter layer.

Can we use our own AI tools?+

Yes. Give your team's ChatGPT or Claude access to PromptVu, brief it on your product and the meeting, and it applies the prompt changes. Reps review before the call.
