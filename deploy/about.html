About PromptVu

People don't need more information, just a better way to deliver it

PromptVu is a human performance aid. We take what your systems already know and put it in front of your people, in real time, while they're in the conversation that matters.

Why we exist

Most software is overbuilt and underused. We wanted to build the opposite of that feeling.

Most of what teams pay for never makes it into the conversation.

Companies have never had more tools. A CRM full of deal history. Call recordings for every meeting. AI that can write a tailored talk track in seconds. Training, playbooks, product docs, customer research.

And yet, when the screen share starts, the person presenting is mostly on their own. All of that knowledge lives somewhere other than the conversation. The value stalls right before it reaches the buyer.

That's the gap we care about. Not storing more data. Not adding another dashboard. Getting what you already know to the person who needs it, at the moment they need it.

How we think about it

We don't replace your tools or your AI. We're the last step between them and the live conversation.

The cost of the gap

Companies solve a delivery problem with headcount

When no one person can carry everything into the room, teams stack people on the deal. It works, but it's expensive and hard to scale, and it still depends on who shows up.

Why it matters now

Most AI is built to replace people, and we're building it to make them indispensable

Every week brings a new promise to automate someone out of the conversation. We think that misses what actually wins deals, keeps customers and builds trust: a person who understands the problem, reads the room and connects. PromptVu doesn't do the talking. It makes sure the person who does has everything they need, so they become the one the business can't do without.
