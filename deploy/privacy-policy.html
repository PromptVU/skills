PromptVu Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 11, 2026
Last Updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PromptVu LLC (“PromptVu,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), a Utah limited liability company, collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects information when users access our websites, applications, software, browser-based products, AI tools, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

By accessing or using PromptVu, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree, do not use the Services.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

PromptVu intentionally limits the amount of personal information we collect.

We may collect:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password credentials (encrypted/authenticated through third-party systems where applicable)
  • Billing-related account information

User Created Content

Users may voluntarily input:

  • Prompts
  • Scripts
  • Notes
  • Presentation materials typed into the platform

Users are solely responsible for the content they input.

Call Audio and Transcripts (Copilot)

Copilot is off by default. If you turn it on, PromptVu captures audio from your call while Copilot is running. This includes your microphone and the other people on the call.

We use this audio to:

  • Transcribe the call
  • Generate live suggestions on your screen

We store the call audio and the transcript with your account. When you turn Copilot off, PromptVu stops capturing audio.

Many states and countries require you to tell everyone on a call that it is being recorded, and some require their consent. If you turn on Copilot, you are responsible for giving that notice and getting any consent the law requires.

Payment Information

Payments are processed through Stripe.

PromptVu does not store full payment card numbers.

Please review Stripe’s Privacy Policy for their data practices.

Technical Information

We may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Browser information
  • Operating system information
  • Crash logs
  • Error logs
  • Usage analytics
  • Login timestamps

This helps us maintain functionality and security.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages they visit and which buttons they click. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your IP address, browser type and pages viewed. We use this information only to improve our website. You can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout or by blocking cookies in your browser.

2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

PromptVu does not intentionally collect:

  • Screen recordings
  • Video recordings
  • Keystroke recordings
  • Browsing history
  • Passwords entered into third-party websites
  • Webcam footage

The only audio PromptVu collects is call audio captured by Copilot, and only when you turn it on (see Section 1).

PromptVu is not surveillance software.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We may use information to:

  • Provide platform functionality
  • Store user prompts
  • Store user notes
  • Transcribe calls and generate live suggestions when Copilot is on
  • Improve platform performance
  • Process payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud
  • Monitor abuse
  • Improve AI functionality
  • Maintain platform security

4. AI PROCESSING

PromptVu uses OpenAI APIs for certain AI-powered functionality.

When Copilot is on, call audio and transcripts are processed by our AI and transcription service providers to create the transcript and suggestions.

Users acknowledge:

  • AI outputs may be inaccurate
  • AI outputs may be incomplete
  • AI outputs may contain errors

Users are responsible for reviewing AI-generated outputs before using them.

PromptVu may use anonymized, aggregated, or de-identified user activity and content data to improve platform performance and future product functionality.

PromptVu reserves the right to expand AI capabilities in the future.

5. USER RESPONSIBILITY FOR CONTENT

Users are fully responsible for any information they enter into PromptVu.

This includes sensitive information related to:

  • healthcare
  • finance
  • legal matters
  • government matters
  • education records
  • confidential company information

PromptVu does not verify whether users are authorized to input such information.

Users assume all responsibility for any sensitive information entered into the platform.

6. DATA STORAGE

We store user prompts, scripts, notes and, when Copilot is on, call audio and transcripts for platform functionality.

Users are responsible for deleting their own content if they no longer wish it to remain stored.

PromptVu is not responsible for retaining deleted user content.

7. DATA DELETION

Users may delete their own prompts, scripts, notes, call audio and transcripts through platform functionality where available, or by contacting us.

PromptVu may retain backup logs, legal records, fraud prevention logs, or billing records as necessary.

8. SHARING OF INFORMATION

PromptVu does not sell personal information.
